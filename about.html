Maruleng Football Club

Who We Are

Football Rooted in Maruleng

Maruleng Football Club is a football club based in Ga-Sekororo, Mabins Cross, Limpopo.

Established in 2012, the club represents its local community through football and continues to build its identity around teamwork, discipline and commitment.

Maruleng FC provides a platform for players to compete, develop their football abilities and represent their community with pride.
