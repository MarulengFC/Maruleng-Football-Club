About the Partnership

Mpho Web Solutions is responsible for the development and digital presentation of the Maruleng Football Club website.

The website provides supporters, players, members and the wider community with access to official club information in one place.

The platform includes information about the club, team profiles, fixtures, results, news, photographs and other club activities.
